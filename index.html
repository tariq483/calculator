About Smart Calculator

Smart Calculator brings four tools into one page: a standard calculator for everyday arithmetic, a scientific mode for trigonometry, logarithms and powers, a percentage tool for quick comparisons, and a discount calculator for working out sale prices. Every result is added to your history automatically, so you can retrace a chain of calculations without redoing the math.

Accurate by default

Standard order of operations, decimal-safe math and built-in protection against dividing by zero.

Works offline

No sign-in, no server and no tracking. History and theme are stored only in this browser.

Keyboard friendly

Type numbers and operators directly, press Enter for the result and Escape to clear.
